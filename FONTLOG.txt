FONTLOG for Pentas Meong!

This file provides detailed information on the Pentas Meong! Font Software.
This information should be distributed along with the Pentas Meong! fonts and
any derivative works.


Basic Font Information

Pentas Meong! is a quirky condensed sans for posters, websites and music and art
headlines that still works in running text. It has eight weights (ExtraLight to
Black) on a single weight axis, true small caps, a curved-arm K, and
wedge-shaped commas and quotes. The classic forms are kept as stylistic sets.

Pentas Meong! was inspired by the text face of Lomba Sihir's album Obrolan Jam
3 Pagi: the typeface used for its lyrics and other text, alongside the album's
fantail display lettering.

Pentas Meong! is a modified version of Reddit Sans Condensed by Stephen
Hutchings / Reddit, Inc. (https://github.com/reddit/redditsans), licensed under
the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Reddit Sans declares no Reserved Font Name.


ChangeLog

3 Nov 2026 (The Pentas Meong! Project Authors) Pentas Meong! 1.000
- New family name "Pentas Meong!". PostScript and file names use the slug
  PentasMeongSeru in all static, variable and SC fonts.
- Capitals narrowed in the heavier weights (stem weight preserved) and re-spaced,
  with extra room around round and diagonal letters
- Lowercase and figures narrowed in the heavier weights to match the capitals;
  original lowercase spacing kept
- Bars and slashes of Ħ, Ø, ø and the slashed zero shortened to match the
  narrower letters; accent positions and bars at intermediate weights (brace
  layers) adjusted to match
- Ink traps on N, M, W, v, w and y kept from Reddit Sans
- New curved-arm K and k (default); the original forms are in ss05 "Straight K"
- New slanted wedge comma and quotes (default); the original forms are in ss06
  "Classic punctuation"
- Added 259 small caps (.sc / .c2sc): a-z with all accented forms, æ œ þ ə ß ð ħ
  ĸ ł ŋ ø ŧ, figures and & ! ? ¡ ¿, with smcp and c2sc features
- Added Ǵ (U+01F4), the capital of ǵ, which Reddit Sans lacked
- į and ị now lose their dot when a top accent is added (e.g. Lithuanian į́, ị̃),
  as i already did
- Five unused glyphs (NULL and orphaned alternates/marks) are no longer
  exported
- cpsp (capital spacing) feature with weight-dependent poster tracking for
  capitals and small caps
- Kerning updated for the new shapes and spacing; small-cap kerning added.
  The new comma and quotes, the straight quotes, and the ss05/ss06 classic
  forms each have their own kerning groups. Kerning pairs that referred to empty
  groups were removed.
- ExtraLight and Black static fonts are now exported
- Builds a "Pentas Meong! SC" companion family (small caps as lowercase)
