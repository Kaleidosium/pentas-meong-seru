FONTLOG for Pentas Meong Seru

This file provides detailed information on the Pentas Meong Seru Font Software.
This information should be distributed along with the Pentas Meong Seru fonts and
any derivative works.


Basic Font Information

Pentas Meong Seru is a quirky condensed sans for posters, websites and music and art
headlines that still works in running text. It has eight weights (ExtraLight to
Black) on a single weight axis, true small caps, a K with an h-shaped leg, a 4
with a full-height diagonal over a short stem, an epsilon-style ampersand, a
raised eight-armed asterisk, slanted commas and wedge-shaped quotes. The
classic forms are kept as stylistic sets.
Its brand name is written "Pentas Meong!"; the family name spells the "!" out
as "Seru" (the exclamation mark is "tanda seru" in Indonesian).

Pentas Meong Seru was inspired by the text face of Lomba Sihir's album Obrolan Jam
3 Pagi: the typeface used for its lyrics and other text, alongside the album's
fantail display lettering.

Pentas Meong Seru is a modified version of Reddit Sans Condensed by Stephen
Hutchings / Reddit, Inc. (https://github.com/reddit/redditsans), licensed under
the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Reddit Sans declares no Reserved Font Name.


ChangeLog

4 Nov 2026 (The Pentas Meong Seru Project Authors) Pentas Meong Seru 1.001
- Family renamed from "Pentas Meong!" to "Pentas Meong Seru" (and "Pentas Meong
  Seru SC"), because family names can't contain "!". File and PostScript names
  are unchanged (PentasMeongSeru). The specimens keep the "Pentas Meong!" brand.
- Small caps for the micro sign, florin, liter sign and pi (smcp; the symbols
  keep their shape, with small-cap spacing) and a small-cap Ohm sign (c2sc)
- Ligature carets for the small-cap fi and fl
- The ss01/cv03 d with caron no longer puts its caron over the next letter
  around the Regular weight (a misplaced component inherited from Reddit Sans)
- Static TTFs are hinted with Reddit Sans' ttfautohint settings again (12 to 32
  ppem, x-height snapping exceptions at 12-14 ppem) plus the strong stem-width
  mode
- New meta table (Latin). The SC family no longer has an smcp feature, which
  had no effect there.
- The static SC OTFs now have their own CFF names. They had kept the main
  family's (e.g. PentasMeongSeru-Bold), so apps that identify fonts by them
  could mix up the two families.
- New contextual alternates (calt, on by default; reddit/redditsans#18):
  3x9 becomes 3 multiplied by 9, 12:34 gets a raised colon, -> <- <-> become
  arrows, and brackets, dashes, @, slashes, middle dot and bullet switch to
  their case forms next to capitals and figures
- New raised colon (colon.case), also used by the case feature

Design:
- New K (capital and small cap): the leg curves down from the stem like an h,
  with a straight arm to the top right. ss05 "Straight K" now changes only the
  capital and small-cap K.
- The lowercase k is straight again by default; the curved-arm k moved to
  cv19 "Curly k"
- New eight-armed asterisk with even strokes, centred on the cap height (the
  low asterisk, two-asterisk and asterism glyphs keep their positions)
- New ampersand: a domed top with a short hook, a V-notched waist, and a
  crossbar that sticks out past the bowl, split by a narrow gap that opens the
  lower counter, as on the Lomba Sihir poster (also the small-cap ampersand).
  The top shoulder and the lower bowl no longer thin out at the light weights
  (their inner corners now follow the outer curves). It is re-centred in its
  width (more room on the left, less on the right) and kerns with following
  capitals, diagonals, quotes and punctuation.
- Four: a new 4. A straight, full-height diagonal with a flat top runs down
  to a low crossbar, and the stem stops just above the middle, so the 4 is
  open at the top right. The crossbar ends at the stem, and the 4 is as wide
  as the other figures. It covers lining, old-style, tabular, small-cap,
  superior, inferior and fraction figures and kerns like the classic 4 on its
  diagonal side. The bent-arm open 4 moved to cv20 "Bent 4".
- New 6 and 9 with straight diagonals, in lining, old-style, tabular,
  small-cap, numerator and denominator styles. The previous 4, 6 and 9 are in
  ss07 "Classic figures".
- Quotes: stronger wedges, so opening and closing quotes differ at text sizes
  (single, double and low-9 quotes, and the modifier apostrophes). The comma
  keeps its parallelogram.
- Math signs (+ - x / = != +- < > <= >= not and approx-equal) share one width;
  the approx-equal sign is narrower to fit.
- Kerning groups updated for the new K, 4 and ampersand; kerning pairs left
  without glyphs were removed
- Designer: Dania Rifki added next to Stephen Hutchings.

New letters, with small caps: IJ ij, U and u with caron, long s, G and g with
stroke, H and h with caron, K and k with caron, Ezh and ezh, Ezh and ezh with
caron (the Dutch, French and German auxiliary letters and the Sami letters used
in Finland).

Fixes, most of them inherited from Reddit Sans:
- ss05 "Straight K" also turned the lowercase k into the curly k. The curly k
  is now only in cv19.
- The f stem sat 2 units above the baseline at ExtraLight (f, fi, fl, ff...).
- The numerator, superior, inferior and subscript 4 were 36 units too wide;
  the inferior 1 at ExtraLight and two other composites had stale widths.
- The slash of the ss01 o-slash sat 2 units high at Regular.
- Nodes 1 or 2 units off the baseline, x-height or cap height in 19 glyphs,
  including r, the old-style tabular 4, 5 and 7, arrows, summation, infinity,
  empty set, currency, dong, plus and divide.
- Overlapping contours merged in the small-cap x, 8, the old-style tabular 8,
  infinity, the arrows and the single-storey a; X and x no longer have
  coincident segments; the micro sign's descender no longer shares an edge
  with the u. Infinity and the single-storey a had 2-unit slips at ExtraLight.
- The l of the ss01 fl ligature reached 1 unit above the cap height.
- Points added where a few curves cross the baseline or cap height (old-style
  3, inferior 0, liter sign, ezh, classic comma, double breve), so the
  TrueType conversion no longer leaves points 1 or 2 units off those lines.
- The one-quarter and three-quarters fractions now store their real component
  positions instead of relying on stale ones.
- OFL.txt: both copyright notices on its first line, as in the fonts.

3 Nov 2026 (The Pentas Meong! Project Authors) Pentas Meong! 1.000
- New family name "Pentas Meong!". PostScript and file names use the slug
  PentasMeongSeru in all static, variable and SC fonts.
- Capitals narrowed in the heavier weights (stem weight preserved) and re-spaced,
  with extra room around round and diagonal letters
- Lowercase and figures narrowed in the heavier weights to match the capitals;
  original lowercase spacing kept
- Bars and slashes of Ħ, Ø, ø and the slashed zero shortened to match the
  narrower letters; accent positions and bars at intermediate weights (brace
  layers) adjusted to match
- Ink traps on N, M, W, v, w and y kept from Reddit Sans
- New curved-arm K and k (default); the original forms are in ss05 "Straight K"
- New slanted wedge comma and quotes (default); the original forms are in ss06
  "Classic punctuation"
- Added 270 small caps (.sc / .c2sc): a-z with all accented forms, æ œ þ ə ß ð ħ
  ĸ ł ŋ ø ŧ, the fi/fl ligatures, the digraphs Ǆ ǅ ǆ Ǉ ǈ ǉ Ǌ ǋ ǌ, figures and
  & ! ? ¡ ¿, with smcp and c2sc features
- Added Ǵ (U+01F4), the capital of ǵ, which Reddit Sans lacked
- Added the line and paragraph separators (U+2028, U+2029); the zero-width
  space (U+200B) now has zero width
- Tabular figures no longer kern
- į and ị now lose their dot when a top accent is added (e.g. Lithuanian į́, ị̃),
  as i already did
- Five unused glyphs (NULL and orphaned alternates/marks) are no longer
  exported
- cpsp (capital spacing) feature with weight-dependent poster tracking for
  capitals and small caps
- Kerning updated for the new shapes and spacing; small-cap kerning added.
  The new comma and quotes, the straight quotes, and the ss05/ss06 classic
  forms each have their own kerning groups. Kerning pairs that referred to empty
  groups were removed.
- ExtraLight and Black static fonts are now exported
- Builds a "Pentas Meong! SC" companion family (small caps as lowercase)
