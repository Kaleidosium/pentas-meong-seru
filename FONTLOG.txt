FONTLOG for Pentas Meong Seru

This file provides detailed information on the Pentas Meong Seru Font Software.
This information should be distributed along with the Pentas Meong Seru fonts and
any derivative works.


Basic Font Information

Pentas Meong Seru is a quirky sans serif for gig posters, websites, and music
and art headlines. It also works in running text. Its capitals put
near-circular rounds beside narrow, straight-sided letters. It has eight
weights, from ExtraLight to Black, on one weight axis, and true small caps.
Other traits set it apart: a K whose leg curves like an h, a 4 with a
full-height diagonal over a short stem, an epsilon-style ampersand, a raised
eight-armed asterisk, slanted commas, square-headed quotes and a flat-topped
question mark. Stylistic sets keep the classic forms.

The brand name is written "Pentas Meong!". Font family names can't contain
punctuation, so the family name spells the "!" out as "Seru", from "tanda
seru", the Indonesian name for the exclamation mark.

The inspiration for Pentas Meong Seru is the text face of Lomba Sihir's album
Obrolan Jam 3 Pagi. That is the typeface used for the album's lyrics and
other text, next to its fantail display lettering. The traits listed above
are Pentas Meong Seru's own take on ideas from that face. Pentas Meong Seru
isn't meant as a one-to-one recreation of the Lomba Sihir font. The starting
point for its outlines is Reddit Sans Condensed.

Pentas Meong Seru is a modified version of Reddit Sans Condensed by Stephen
Hutchings for Reddit, Inc. (https://github.com/reddit/redditsans), licensed
under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Reddit Sans Condensed declares no
Reserved Font Name.


ChangeLog

21 Nov 2026 (The Pentas Meong Seru Project Authors) Pentas Meong Seru 1.000

First release. These are the changes from Reddit Sans Condensed.

Families and fonts
- Two families, Pentas Meong Seru and Pentas Meong Seru SC. The SC family
  encodes small caps directly at the lowercase code points, so it works in
  apps that can't turn OpenType features on. It has no smcp or c2sc feature.
- Eight weights from ExtraLight to Black, as variable fonts and as static TTF
  and OTF fonts. The main family also comes as variable and static WOFF2
  fonts. PostScript names and file names use PentasMeongSeru.
- The static TTFs keep Reddit Sans Condensed's ttfautohint settings, 12 to 32
  pixels per em, and add the strong stem-width mode. Alternates and small
  caps share the alignment zones of the letters around them. ExtraBold and
  Black round the x-height up at 14 pixels per em, which keeps the dots of
  i, j and ! clear of the stem. The dot of the question marks stays clear of
  the stem except in the ExtraBold and Black small caps at 12 and 13 pixels
  per em. The static OTFs are hinted with otfautohint, and the variable fonts
  are unhinted.
- The underline matches the underscore and the strikeout matches the hyphen,
  at every weight.
- The ExtraLight OTFs use weight class 275, because older Windows apps
  embolden CFF fonts lighter than 250. A meta table declares Latin.
- Dania Rifki is credited as designer next to Stephen Hutchings.

Design
- The heavier weights are narrower, with the stem weights kept. The capitals
  are re-spaced, with extra room around round and diagonal letters. The
  lowercase keeps its spacing.
- Round letters are close to circles. The capital O, C, Q, Schwa and OE are
  much wider, D is a little wider, and G is about as wide as C. The lowercase
  and small-cap rounds are widened less, and a u n h m r s widen with the o.
  The figures keep their widths.
- The leg of the K curves down from the stem like an h, in capitals and small
  caps. The straight K is in ss05 "Straight K". A lowercase k with a curved
  arm is in cv19 "Curly k".
- The 4 has a straight, full-height diagonal over a low crossbar and a short
  stem, which leaves it open at the top right. The 6 and 9 have straight
  diagonals. These shapes cover every figure style. The classic 4, 6 and 9
  are in ss07 "Classic figures", and the bent-arm open 4 is in cv20 "Bent 4".
- The ampersand has a domed top, a V-notched waist and a split crossbar,
  inspired by the one on the Lomba Sihir poster. The classic ampersand is in
  ss08 "Classic ampersand".
- The asterisk has eight even arms and is centred on the cap height.
- The comma is slanted. The quotes have square heads and a tail at the
  comma's slant. The question mark has a flat top and a round bowl that opens
  to the left. The interrobang has a slanted gap at the top, which keeps it
  from reading as a P. The classic round punctuation is in ss06 "Classic
  punctuation", and a wider question mark is in ss09 "Wide question mark".
- The math signs share one width.
- The case feature raises the colon, the semicolon, the inverted question and
  exclamation marks and the less common dashes.

Small caps and new characters
- Small caps (smcp and c2sc) cover a to z with every accented form, the other
  Latin letters, the f ligatures, the DZ, LJ and NJ digraphs, the figures and
  & ! ? ¡ ¿. Omega, Delta and Pi and the ss03 barred I and tall W have small
  caps too.
- New letters, all with small caps: IJ and ij, long s, G and g with stroke,
  H, K, U and Ezh with caron, Ezh, Ǵ, O with dot above, O with caron, the U
  with dieresis forms that complete Hanyu Pinyin, D with cedilla for
  Livonian, the dotted consonants of older Irish spelling, Kurdish Ḧ and Ẍ,
  F with hook, Latin alpha and n with long right leg.
- Greek capital Mu, Pi, Delta and Omega and small delta and omega, so a case
  change of µ, π, Δ and Ω stays in the font.
- The line and paragraph separators and the narrow no-break space.

Features and kerning
- cpsp adds poster tracking to capitals, small caps and lining figures, and
  the tracking grows with the weight.
- calt (reddit/redditsans#18) turns 3x9 into 3×9 and -> <- <-> into arrows. It
  raises the colon in 12:34, raises dashes, @, slashes, the middle dot and the
  bullet next to capitals and figures, and raises brackets as a pair around
  capitals and figures. Hexadecimal numbers such as 0x1F keep their x, and
  brackets around mixed-case text stay low.
- New stylistic sets and character variants: ss05 to ss09, cv19 and cv20. All
  stylistic sets and character variants have descriptive names. The circular
  zero (ss04, cv18) also works with old-style figures.
- The default r/ ligature is removed, because it tightened every r before a
  slash in ordinary text.
- Tabular figures don't kern.
- The kerning is redone for the new shapes and spacing, and small caps have
  their own kerning. Accents, horns and ogoneks keep clear of the letters and
  punctuation next to them at every weight.

Fixes to problems inherited from Reddit Sans Condensed
- Points that sat 1 or 2 units off the baseline, x-height or cap height,
  overlapping contours, stale component positions and widths, and kinks in
  the 2 are fixed.
- The figure dash, horizontal bar, math angle brackets, pi and tabular 0, 2
  and 7 now match the weight, height or width of the glyphs around them. The
  hairline cracks in the won sign and the interrobang are closed.
- Combining marks stack centred above other marks. The combining cedilla and
  ogonek attach to every letter that stands on the baseline. Accents on small
  caps and capitals are centred at every weight, and the f ligatures have an
  anchor for each letter.
- Pairs that collided or sat loose are kerned. Among them are the accented o
  and e, Ư, ľ, the accented small-cap and cursive i after capitals, ≤ ≥ < >
  next to accents, the stars, and slashes after ogoneks.
- The ffi and ffl ligatures follow ss01 and ss02 like fi and fl. dlig only
  forms the small-cap fi and fl. ﬀ ﬃ ﬄ are encoded.
- In Black, the small-cap 0 has an open counter, and ! ? ¡ ¿ ‽ ⁂ ≤ ≥ and ₫
  have wider gaps. The dieresis sits a little higher in ExtraBold and Black.
- The zero-width space has zero width, the hair space is a third of the thin
  space, and the carriage return is as wide as the space.
- Eight unused glyphs are no longer exported.
