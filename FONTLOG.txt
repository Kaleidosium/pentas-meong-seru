FONTLOG for Pentas Meong Seru

This file provides detailed information on the Pentas Meong Seru Font Software.
This information should be distributed along with the Pentas Meong Seru fonts and
any derivative works.


Basic Font Information

Pentas Meong Seru is a quirky sans for posters, websites and music and art
headlines that still works in running text. Its capitals set near-circular
rounds beside narrow straight-sided letters. It has eight weights (ExtraLight to
Black) on a single weight axis, true small caps, a K with an h-shaped leg, a 4
with a full-height diagonal over a short stem, an epsilon-style ampersand, a
raised eight-armed asterisk, slanted commas and wedge-shaped quotes. The
classic forms are kept as stylistic sets.
Its brand name is written "Pentas Meong!"; the family name spells the "!" out
as "Seru" (the exclamation mark is "tanda seru" in Indonesian).

Pentas Meong Seru was inspired by the text face of Lomba Sihir's album Obrolan Jam
3 Pagi: the typeface used for its lyrics and other text, alongside the album's
fantail display lettering.

Pentas Meong Seru is a modified version of Reddit Sans Condensed by Stephen
Hutchings / Reddit, Inc. (https://github.com/reddit/redditsans), licensed under
the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Reddit Sans declares no Reserved Font Name.


ChangeLog

7 Nov 2026 (The Pentas Meong Seru Project Authors) Pentas Meong Seru 1.000

First release. Changes from Reddit Sans Condensed:

Families and fonts:
- Two families: "Pentas Meong Seru" and "Pentas Meong Seru SC", in which the
  lowercase is small caps (for apps that can't switch OpenType features on;
  it has no smcp or c2sc).
  PostScript and file names use PentasMeongSeru.
- Eight weights, ExtraLight to Black, as variable fonts and as static TTF, OTF
  and WOFF2 fonts.
- The static TTFs are hinted with Reddit Sans' ttfautohint settings (12 to 32
  ppem, x-height snapping exceptions at 12-14 ppem) plus the strong stem-width
  mode. The variable fonts are unhinted.
- A meta table declares Latin.
- Designer: Dania Rifki added next to Stephen Hutchings.

Design:
- In the heavier weights the capitals, lowercase and figures are narrower, with
  the stem weights kept. The capitals are re-spaced, with extra room around
  round and diagonal letters; the lowercase keeps its spacing.
- Round letters are close to circles. The capital O, C, Q, Schwa and OE (with
  all their accented forms) are much wider, D is a little wider, and G is about
  as wide as C. The lowercase and small-cap rounds (o c e g, b d p q thorn,
  ae oe, eth, the single-storey a and the ss01 forms) are widened less, and
  a u n h m r s are widened by the same amount as o so they stay even with it.
  The figures are not widened, and 0 stays narrow.
- Bars and slashes (H with stroke, O with stroke, the slashed zero) and accent
  positions follow the new widths, in the intermediate masters too.
- The ink traps on N, M, W, v, w and y are kept from Reddit Sans.
- K (capital and small cap): the leg curves down from the stem like an h, with
  a straight arm to the top right. The straight K is in ss05 "Straight K". The
  lowercase k stays straight; a k with a curved arm is in cv19 "Curly k".
- 4: a straight, full-height diagonal with a flat top runs down to a low
  crossbar that ends at the stem, and the stem stops just above the middle, so
  the 4 is open at the top right. It is a little narrower than the other
  figures. This covers lining, old-style, tabular, small-cap, superior,
  inferior and fraction figures. The bent-arm open 4 is in cv20 "Bent 4".
- 6 and 9 with straight diagonals, in lining, old-style, tabular, small-cap,
  numerator and denominator styles. The classic 4, 6 and 9 are in ss07
  "Classic figures".
- Ampersand: a domed top with a short hook, a V-notched waist, and a crossbar
  that sticks out past the bowl, split by a narrow gap that opens the lower
  counter, as on the Lomba Sihir poster (also the small-cap ampersand). In
  Black, thinner horizontals and a wider gap keep the counters open at text
  sizes.
- Asterisk: eight arms with even strokes, centred on the cap height, with
  heavier arms in Black. The low asterisk, two-asterisk and asterism glyphs
  keep their positions.
- Punctuation: a slanted comma and wedge-shaped quotes, so opening and closing
  quotes differ at text sizes (also the semicolon, the single, double and
  low-9 quotes and the modifier apostrophes). In ExtraLight they are thinner,
  to match the text. The classic round forms are in ss06 "Classic
  punctuation".
- Math signs (+ - x / = != +- < > <= >= not and approx-equal) share one width;
  the approx-equal sign is narrower to fit.
- A raised colon (colon.case), used by the case and calt features.

Small caps and new characters:
- Small caps (smcp and c2sc): a-z with all their accented forms, æ œ þ ə ß ð
  ħ ĸ ł ŋ ø ŧ, the fi and fl ligatures (with ligature carets), the digraphs
  Ǆ ǅ ǆ Ǉ ǈ ǉ Ǌ ǋ ǌ, figures and & ! ? ¡ ¿. The micro sign, florin, liter
  sign and pi keep their shape with small-cap spacing, and the Ohm sign has a
  small cap (c2sc).
- New letters, with small caps: IJ ij, U and u with caron, long s, G and g
  with stroke, H and h with caron, K and k with caron, Ezh and ezh, Ezh and
  ezh with caron (the Dutch, French and German auxiliary letters and the Sami
  letters used in Finland).
- Ǵ (U+01F4), the capital of ǵ, which Reddit Sans lacked.
- The line and paragraph separators (U+2028, U+2029).

Features and kerning:
- cpsp (capital spacing): poster tracking for capitals and small caps that
  scales with the weight.
- Contextual alternates (calt, on by default; reddit/redditsans#18): 3x9
  becomes 3 multiplied by 9 (also 10x10 and 1920x1080, and with small caps),
  while hexadecimal numbers such as 0x1F keep their x; 12:34 gets a raised
  colon; -> <- <-> become arrows, while x<-1 stays as typed; and brackets,
  dashes, @, slashes, middle dot and bullet switch to their case forms next
  to capitals and figures.
- New stylistic sets and character variants: ss05 "Straight K", ss06 "Classic
  punctuation", ss07 "Classic figures", cv19 "Curly k" and cv20 "Bent 4".
- Tabular figures don't kern.
- į and ị lose their dot when a top accent is added (e.g. Lithuanian į́, ị̃),
  as i already did.
- Kerning updated for the new shapes and spacing, with small-cap kerning. The
  new comma and quotes, the straight quotes and the ss05, ss06 and ss07 forms
  have their own kerning groups. Kerning pairs that referred to empty groups
  or missing glyphs were removed.

Fixes to problems inherited from Reddit Sans:
- The ss01/cv03 d with caron put its caron over the next letter around the
  Regular weight (a misplaced component), and the ss01 u with double acute
  had a narrower advance than its u at Regular.
- The f stem sat 2 units above the baseline at ExtraLight (f, fi, fl, ff...).
- The inferior 1 at ExtraLight and two other composites had stale widths.
- The slash of the ss01 o-slash sat 2 units high at Regular.
- Nodes 1 or 2 units off the baseline, x-height or cap height in 19 glyphs,
  including r, the old-style tabular 4, 5 and 7, arrows, summation, infinity,
  empty set, currency, dong, plus and divide.
- Overlapping contours merged in the old-style tabular 8, infinity, the arrows,
  pi and the single-storey a; X and x no longer have coincident segments; the
  micro sign's descender no longer shares an edge with the u. Infinity and the
  single-storey a had 2-unit slips at ExtraLight.
- The l of the ss01 fl ligature reached 1 unit above the cap height.
- Points added where a few curves cross the baseline or cap height (old-style
  3, inferior 0, liter sign, ezh, classic comma, double breve), so the
  TrueType conversion no longer leaves points 1 or 2 units off those lines.
  The superior 3 and the dot of Ṧ no longer land 2 units off the cap height
  and ascender.
- Around the Medium and SemiBold weights, the zeros of % and ‰ and the acute
  and grave accents were drawn 12-17% wider than the Regular and Black
  masters allow (intermediate masters left over from the wider Reddit Sans),
  so % reached past its advance width there.
- The one-quarter and three-quarters fractions store their real component
  positions instead of relying on stale ones.
- The zero-width space (U+200B) has zero width.
- Five unused glyphs (NULL and orphaned alternates and marks) are no longer
  exported.
- The cv12 name is spelled "Single-storey a".
