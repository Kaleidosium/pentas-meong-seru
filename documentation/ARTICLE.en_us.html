Pentas Meong Seru is a quirky sans serif for gig posters, websites, and music and art headlines. Its heavy weights take their cue from hand-set gig posters, with near-circular round capitals beside narrow straight ones and generous tracking. The lighter weights are calm enough for running text, so one family can set a headline and the small print under it. To contribute, see github.com/kaleidosium/pentas-meong-seru.

The design was inspired by the text face of Lomba Sihir's album Obrolan Jam 3 Pagi, the typeface used for its lyrics and other text alongside the album's fantail display lettering. Several traits are Pentas Meong Seru's own take on ideas from that face: a K whose leg curves down from the stem like an h, a 4 with a full-height diagonal over a short stem, an epsilon-style ampersand, a raised eight-armed asterisk, slanted commas, square-headed quotes and a flat-topped question mark. Pentas Meong Seru isn't meant as a one-to-one recreation of the Lomba Sihir font.

The starting point for the outlines is Reddit Sans Condensed, designed by Stephen Hutchings for Reddit, Inc. Pentas Meong Seru has since moved well away from it. Its round letters are close to circles, its heavy weights are narrower, its capitals are re-spaced, and many of its glyphs are new or redrawn.

Features

The family has eight weights, from ExtraLight to Black, on a variable weight axis. It covers the Google Fonts Latin Core and Latin Vietnamese glyph sets. Its true small caps include every accented letter, the figures and & ! ? ¡ ¿. A capital spacing feature adds poster tracking to capitals, small caps and lining figures, and the tracking grows with the weight.

Contextual alternates are on by default. They turn 3x9 into 3×9 and -> into an arrow, raise the colon in 12:34, and raise brackets as a pair around capitals and figures, as in (OK) and (2026).

Stylistic sets offer geometric lowercase forms, a single-storey a, a cursive i, a two-storey g, a barred I, a tall W, a circular zero, a curly k, a bent 4 and a wide question mark for display. Other sets bring back the classic forms of Reddit Sans Condensed: a straight-armed K, round punctuation, the classic 4, 6 and 9, and the classic ampersand.

A companion family, Pentas Meong Seru SC, has small caps in place of the lowercase, for apps that can't turn OpenType features on.

The name

The brand name, Pentas Meong!, ends in an exclamation mark. Font family names can't contain punctuation, so the family name spells the "!" out as "Seru", from tanda seru, the Indonesian name for the exclamation mark.
