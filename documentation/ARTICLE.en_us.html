Pentas Meong Seru is a quirky sans serif for posters, websites and music and art headlines. Its heavy weights take their cue from hand-set gig posters: near-circular round capitals beside narrow straight ones, generous tracking, a K with an h-shaped leg, a short-stemmed 4, an epsilon-style ampersand, slanted commas and wedge-shaped quotes. The lighter weights stay calm and readable, so the same family can set a headline and the text underneath it.

Pentas Meong Seru was inspired by the text face of Lomba Sihir’s album Obrolan Jam 3 Pagi: the typeface used for its lyrics and other text, alongside the album’s fantail display lettering.

The family has eight weights, from ExtraLight to Black, on a variable weight axis. It includes true small caps (with all accented letters, figures and punctuation), a weight-aware capital spacing feature for poster settings, and stylistic sets that bring back a straight-armed K, classic figures and classic round punctuation. A companion family, Pentas Meong Seru SC, uses small caps in place of the lowercase.

Pentas Meong Seru is based on Reddit Sans Condensed by Stephen Hutchings for Reddit, Inc., and is released under the SIL Open Font License.
